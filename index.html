Email → Case → Intent → Route → Handle → Resolve → Measure

Margaret emailed the “wrong” address, yet reached the right specialist in under two minutes, with no forwarding, one case and a complete audit trail.

  1. Email-to-Case turned the email into managed work and kept the attachment and thread on one record.
  2. Copilot intent classification found two intents and a vulnerability indicator, with a confidence score for each.
  3. Intent-led routing sent the work to a skilled team, regardless of which mailbox received it.
  4. A colleague validated the vulnerability indicator, so a person stays accountable on sensitive journeys.
  5. A linked case for the second intent went to its owning team with the full context, without forwarding.
  6. Copilot answers and email drafts were grounded in approved knowledge and reviewed before sending.
  7. Thread correlation attached the customer's reply to the existing case.
  8. Measurement by journey and intent, not by mailbox.
“The email address should become an entry point, not the business process.”
Capability availability (e.g. AI-based intent routing features that may be in preview) must be validated before inclusion in a committed design. Systems of record can remain authoritative.
